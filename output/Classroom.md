Follow these easy steps to join your class:

14. Firewall Issues

1. Use a VPN (Most Likely Fix)

●  Download a free VPN such as Proton VPN or Windscribe.

●  Connect to any available server.

●  Once connected, try joining the Zoom session again from your browser.

This helps bypass network restrictions that may be blocking Zoom.

2. If You Are on a Work/School/College Network

●  Some organizations block Zoom through their firewall.

●  You may need to contact your IT support team and request them to whitelist Zoom domains.

3. Switch to a Mobile Hotspot (Quick Workaround)

1.  Turn on Personal Hotspot on your phone.

2.  Connect your computer to the hotspot.

3.  Try joining Zoom again.

Coaches Extra Info [Internal]

Classroom for coaches

1. How do I start a class in Caissa?
Follow these steps to start your class:

●  Log in to Caissa using the browser [prefer - Google Chrome]
●  Go to the School of Chess tab → click on Meeting
●  Check the meeting name and time, then click Join below Classroom for that meeting

Note : Please don't start a class more than 5 minutes early

2.When I click the Join button, it says there is an active meeting. Why does this happen?
This pop-up appears when:

You didn't properly end your previous meeting, OR
Someone else using the same Zoom ID has a class going on

You will see two options:
●  End and Join

This will end the ongoing meeting and start your new meeting
Use this if:

1.  You forgot to close your previous meeting properly
2.  It's a demo class (check with our team first)

●  Join Anyway

This tries to join without ending the other meeting
If you see "3000 Error - Another meeting in progress", go back and choose End and Join

Tip: Always click End Meeting when your class finishes to avoid this problem next time!

3.What is the difference between Inside Caissa and Floating Window?
When you join a classroom, you will see two options:

Inside Caissa — Zoom opens inside the Caissa window
Floating Window — Zoom opens in a small floating window

Benefits of Floating Window:

It stays open even when you switch screens
You can resize it freely

Important Rules:
Do's:

●  Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (version 116 or higher)
●  Choose your mode at the start of the class

Don'ts:

●  Do NOT switch between modes during class — this will disconnect and reconnect Zoom, which

may disturb your meeting

●  Closing the floating window will move the meeting back inside Caissa (it will reconnect).

4.How do I share the screen?

●  You will be able to see the share screen button if not, please check inside the more option
●  Click Share Screen and choose Particular Window (not Entire Screen)
●  Make sure the window you are sharing is not minimized
●  To see what students are viewing, go to Coach View and click on the green highlight

Tip: Keep your topic positions open on ChessBase before starting!

5.How do I hide the notation bar from students?
To keep the notation open but hidden from students:

●  Click on the notation bar
●  Drag and move it away from the main screen to undock it
●  This way, you can still see the notation, but students will not see it on their screen.

6.How do I create and share a puzzle with students?
To create a puzzle for your students:

●  Copy the position from ChessBase (click the button copy position not copy game in the top left

corner)
●
In Caissa, click Create Puzzle
●  Set the solving time for students
●  Make the moves on the board to set the solution
●  Click Add Puzzle
●  Your students will now see the puzzle and can start solving it!

7.What should I do if I make a wrong move while creating a puzzle?
If you make a wrong move while creating the solution:

●  Close the popup window
●  Start again and remake the puzzle with the correct moves

8.How do I see updated data on the Leaderboard and Past Puzzles?
To see the latest information:

Go to the Leaderboard or Past Puzzles section
Click the Refresh button to load updated data

Note: The leaderboard only displays the top 10 students in the class, and to get into the leaderboard they
should solve at least one puzzle

9.How do I properly end a class?
When your class is finished:

●  Click on End Meeting inside the classroom
●  Do NOT just close the browser tab directly

Why?

Clicking "End Meeting" properly closes the session for everyone. Closing the tab directly may

cause issues for students still in the class.

10.Why is my classroom not opening?
If the classroom doesn't open, try these steps:

●  Use Google Chrome — Always log in using Google Chrome for the best experience
●  Check pop-up settings — The classroom opens in a new window. Make sure your browser is not

blocking pop-ups

●  Allow pop-ups for the Caissa website

Tip: If the window still doesn't open, try refreshing the page and clicking Join again.

Important Information for Coaches Regarding Caissa Classroom Updates

Hi Coaches,

I hope you've had a chance to review the previous message sent to students about the new Caissa
Classroom feature. This follow-up contains key points to help ensure your classes run smoothly and
without interruptions.

Key Points for Coaches:

1.  No Screen Sharing in Caissa Classroom

 Please note that students will not be able to share their screens within the Caissa Classroom app.
So they will not be able to share their homework/assignment during class

2.  Audio Issues

 If a student is unable to hear you after granting the necessary permissions and selecting either
Wi-Fi or Cellular Data, as well as trying with headphones, kindly ask them to join via the Zoom
link instead (just as they would join a regular class). If the issue persists, please have them raise a
support ticket and provide a screenshot for troubleshooting.

3.  Challenge Games During Class

○  What Students Cannot Do:

Students in the app cannot directly go to the League to challenge another player.

○  What Students Can Do:

They can search for a user and challenge them by selecting "Play against CircleChess
User."
If someone challenges them, they can accept the challenge, but they will need to
manually navigate to the "Play" section to start the match.

4.  Full Screen

This will be a little different in app compared to laptop
Turn on Auto Rotate on your phone.
Rotate your phone from portrait → landscape to view Zoom in full screen.
