📌 Sample Feedback Templates (Coach Reference)

Coaches can refer to these based on student type:

1. High Potential Student

Feedback:
[Student Name] is a quick learner and demonstrates strong understanding of concepts. With regular practice and
continued focus, he/she has the potential to perform at a very high level.

Suggestions:

  Solve 20–30 tactical puzzles daily
  Start playing longer time control games (rapid/classical)
  Begin basic opening preparation (White & Black)
  Analyze games after playing to identify mistakes

🔹 2. Conceptually Strong but Underperforming

Feedback:
[Student Name] has a good conceptual understanding but this is not consistently reflected in games/tests, mainly
due to quick decision-making without deep analysis. Slowing down and applying concepts will improve
performance.

Suggestions:

  Spend more time per move and avoid rushing
  Follow a thinking process (Checks, Captures, Threats)
  Play fewer but higher-quality games
  Review mistakes after each game

🔹 3. Speed Issue (Plays Too Fast)

Feedback:
[Student Name] tends to play quickly, which leads to avoidable mistakes. Taking more time to analyze positions
will significantly improve accuracy and results.

Suggestions:

  Shift from blitz/bullet to rapid games
  Pause and double-check moves before playing
  Practice calculation exercises regularly
  Focus on accuracy over speed

🔹 4. Beginner Level

Feedback:
[Student Name] is learning foundational concepts and showing interest in chess. With regular practice and
exposure to basic tactical patterns, steady improvement is expected.

Suggestions:

  Practice basic tactics (forks, pins, skewers)
  Play short games regularly
  Use beginner-friendly practice tools (like Lichess practice)
  Revise concepts learned in class

🔹 5. Needs More Practice

Feedback:
[Student Name] has potential but requires more consistency in practice. Regular puzzle solving and game
analysis will help improve performance.

Suggestions:

  Maintain a daily practice routine (20–30 mins minimum)
  Solve puzzles consistently
  Play 3–4 games per week and review them
  Complete assigned homework regularly

🔹 6. Low Concentration

Feedback:
[Student Name] understands concepts but loses focus during games, often missing opponent threats. Improving
concentration and following a structured thinking process will help.

Suggestions:

  Think from opponent’s perspective before every move
  Follow CCT (Checks, Captures, Threats)
  Avoid distractions during games
  Play fewer games with better focus

🔹 7. Irregular Attendance

Feedback:
[Student Name] shows good understanding but irregular attendance has affected continuity. Regular
participation will help improve consistency and learning.

Suggestions:

  Attend classes regularly
  Revise missed topics through recordings
  Stay consistent with homework
  Maintain a fixed chess practice schedule

🔹 8. No Attendance

Feedback:
[Student Name] has not attended sessions regularly, making it difficult to assess progress at this stage.

Suggestions:

  Start attending classes consistently
  Build a basic routine for learning
  Once regular, structured feedback can be provided

🔹 9. Improving Student

Feedback:
[Student Name] has shown noticeable improvement in recent sessions. Continued effort and consistency will
help maintain this progress.

Suggestions:

  Continue current practice routine

Increase puzzle solving gradually
  Play and analyze games regularly
  Stay consistent with classes and assignments

🔹 10. Advanced / Tournament Player

Feedback:
[Student Name] is progressing well and is ready to focus on advanced areas like openings, positional play, and
game analysis. Tournament exposure is recommended.

Suggestions:

  Build a structured opening repertoire
  Play rated tournaments (online/offline)
  Analyze games deeply
  Study endgames and positional concepts

🔹 11. Confidence Issue

Feedback:
[Student Name] has good understanding but lacks confidence in decision-making. With encouragement and
practice, confidence will improve.

Suggestions:

  Trust own calculations and decisions
  Avoid changing moves repeatedly
  Play more practice games
  Focus on learning, not results

🔹 12. Well-Behaved & Attentive

Feedback:
[Student Name] is attentive, disciplined, and follows instructions well. This is a strong foundation for long-term
improvement.

Suggestions:

  Continue consistent practice

Increase puzzle difficulty gradually
  Start applying concepts more in games
  Take initiative in asking doubts
