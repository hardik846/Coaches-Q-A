PTM Incentive Structure

Base Pay: ₹100 per PTM

Bonus Incentive:
Additional ₹100 per PTM will be provided if all the below conditions are met:

●  Parent attends the PTM
●  Pre-PTM feedback is filled 1 day in advance
●  Post-PTM feedback is submitted

Total pay per PTM:
₹200 (₹100 base + ₹100 bonus)

Important Rule:
PTM will be considered complete only if post-PTM feedback is submitted. Bonus will be
applicable only when all conditions are fulfilled.

Example:
If a coach completes 10 PTMs:

●  Base Pay = ₹1000
●  Bonus (if all conditions met) = ₹1000
●  Total = ₹2000

         Coach Renewal Incentive Policy
We want to reward coaches who keep their students learning! Here is how you earn
extra money every month based on your student renewals.

1. How do we calculate your score?

We look at your Renewal Percentage for the month:

2. How much can you earn?

The more students you retain, the higher your "per-student" reward becomes.

●  0% to 30% Renewal: No extra incentive (This is the baseline).
●  30% to 50% Renewal: You get ₹300 for every renewal above the 30% mark.
●  Above 50% Renewal: You get ₹500 for every renewal above the 30% mark.

The Golden Rule: The first 30% of your renewals are your "base" and don't
pay out. You start making money from the 31st percent onwards

3. The "Must-Do" Condition (PTM)

Even if a student pays, you only get the bonus if a Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM)
happened:

●  Group Classes: Parent must attend at least 2 PTMs
●  1-on-1 Classes: The PTM must be marked as "Held" in the system.
●  No PTM = No Incentive.

4. Deadlines & Cut-offs:

The 15-Day Window: The student must pay within 15 days of their plan expiring. Late
renewals don't count for the bonus.

5. Example

●  Step 1 (The Base): 30% of 20 students = 6 students. (No bonus for these first

6).

●  Step 2 (The Payout): * Scenario A (You get 9 renewals): You get paid for 3

students (9 - 6 = 3) at the ₹300 rate. Total: ₹900.

○  Scenario B (You get 12 renewals): Your renewal rate is now 60% (Above
the 50% slab!). You get paid for 6 students (12 - 6 = 6) at the ₹500 rate.
Total: ₹3,000.
