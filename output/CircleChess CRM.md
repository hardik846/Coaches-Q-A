CircleChess CRM – Guidance

What is CRM  A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system is basically a central
dashboard where a company stores and manages information related to customers, students,
coaches, tasks, follow-ups, and operations.

1.  Coach Manual – The Coach Manual section acts as a quick reference guide for coaches. It
contains important resources and guides that coaches may need during their teaching
journey.

Available Resources:

Classroom and its key features.

4.  PTM Feedback: This section is used for managing all PTM-related activities

A.  Pending PTM Feedback: Whenever a PTM is scheduled, coaches must submit the Pre-

PTM Feedback.
Path:
Give feedback  Edit  Fill form Submit.
This feedback must be submitted at least 24 hours before the PTM.

Important Note

The Coach Recommendation field should always be written professionally.

Since the same recommendation appears in the report card shared with parents, coaches should:

  Use positive and constructive language.
  Avoid negative comments.
  Refer to the sample feedback document whenever required.

6.  Renewal  This section provides details about student renewals for the current and

previous month.

Renewal Status

RENEWED
The student has successfully renewed.

EXPIRED_NOT_RENEWED
The student's subscription has expired and has not yet been renewed.

ALREADY_PAID_AS_LONG_ORDER
The student has already enrolled for a longer duration plan (such as 6 months or 12 months) and will
automatically move to the next renewal cycle when required.

7.  PGNs, Test links & Topics  This section contains teaching resources required for classes.

PGNs : Coaches should use the Classwork PGNs during sessions.

It is recommended to download and organize PGNs level-wise for easy access.

Homework PGNs are uploaded as assignments after the class is conducted.

Test Links: Beginner Test 1 and Beginner Test 2 Google Form links can be accessed here and shared
with students in the classroom chat.

For all other levels, tests are launched using the Publish Test option in the classroom.

Monthly Test Scores - Coaches can view students' monthly test scores here.

Session Topics - Contains the syllabus and session-wise topics for each level.
