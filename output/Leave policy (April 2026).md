        LEAVE & PENALTY POLICY (For Consultants – Hourly Basis)

1. PURPOSE

This policy ensures reliability, punctuality, and consistent class delivery by coaches. It defines the

process for class cancellations, attendance expectations, and associated penalties for non-

compliance.

2. ATTENDANCE & CLASS CANCELLATION POLICY

2.1 Batch-Based Leave Limit

●  Each batch includes 24 scheduled sessions,a coach may cancel a maximum of 2 sessions per

batch (8% of total sessions).

●  Any cancellation beyond this limit will attract the following penalties:

 • 3rd Cancellation →  Penalty of ₹500 of hourly payout

 • 4th Cancellation → Penalty of ₹1000 of hourly payout

 • 5th Cancellation → Penalty of ₹2000 of hourly payout and batch reassignment to another

coach.

2.2 Class Cancellation Notice

● All cancellations must be informed at least 4 hours before the scheduled class start time (for

both 1-on-1 and group sessions).

●  Frequent cancellations or reschedules will affect future batch allocations.

2.3 Penalty Based on Timing of Cancellation

●  More than 4 hours before class start → No penalty

●  Between 4 hours and 1 hour before class → ₹200 Penalty

●  Within 1 hour before class start → ₹500 Penalty

2.4 Steps to Cancel a Class

●  The coach must cancel the class only through the Caissa platform.

●  WhatsApp or email cancellations will not be accepted.

●  If a class is not cancelled through the platform, it will be treated as uninformed leave.

2.5 Uninformed Absence (Missed Sessions Without Approval) (counted monthly)

●  1st instance → Penalty of 2.5x the hourly payout

●  2nd instance → Penalty of 5x the hourly payout

●  3rd instance → Termination of contract

2.6 Late Arrival (More than 3 Minutes Late) (counted monthly)

●  1st instance →  No Penalty

●  2nd instance →  25% deduction of session payout

●  3rd instance →  50% deduction of session payout

●  4th instance → No payout for the session

●  5th instance → No payout + Penalty of 2.5x the hourly payout

2.7 Camera Usage Policy

●  Coaches are required to keep their video camera switched on throughout the session

for both 1-on-1 and group classes.

●

 If the coach’s video remains switched OFF for more than 5 continuous minutes

during a class (without prior approval or valid technical justification), the session will

be considered invalid.

●  No payment will be made for such sessions.

3. POLICY FOR STUDENT NO-SHOW SESSIONS

●  For 1-on-1 sessions where the student does not join, the coach will be eligible for a

50% of the session payout.

●  Coaches must keep the meeting active for 20 minutes for the session to be marked as

“Held.”

●  If it is the student’s first missed session or there appears to be a genuine reason,

coaches may, at their discretion, end the meeting after 10–15 minutes. In such cases,

the session will be marked as “Cancelled.”

●  Coaches are required to raise any conflicts or concerns within 1 day of the session

date.

●  If a coach fails to serve the full notice period, the company reserves the right to hold pending

payments.

8. Availability & Initial Commitment Policy (Applicable for New Coaches Only)

●  No Leave in First Month:

Newly onboarded coaches are not allowed to take any leave during their first 30 days

from the start of their initial batch.

This ensures consistency, student experience, and smooth onboarding.

●  Availability Lock-in:

Once availability is submitted and batches are assigned, coaches cannot change their

availability for a minimum of 6 months.

●  Exceptions:

Any exceptions must be pre-approved by management and may impact future batch

allocations.
