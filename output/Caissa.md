     Caissa – Smart Chess Learning Platform for Kids

What is Caissa?

Caissa is a smart chess learning platform where students learn, practice, analyse, and improve in one place —
with AI insights, structured classes, and interactive tools.

Dashboard Overview (What the child gets): When you open the dashboard, you can see how well you
make decisions, perform under pressure, and build confidence with every move.

1.  Play – Practice games anytime and take part in weekly tournaments

2.  Game Review – Review games and identify mistakes, see better moves you could have played, first
think and find the solution yourself, then engine shows the answer, import games from Lichess and
Chess.com and review them

3.  Assessment Report – Get smart AI Insights on your game

4.  School of Chess – Join classes through Caissa Classroom, watch class recordings, get weekly
assignments, book 1:1 mentor calls, play and learn with peers in CircleChess League

5.  Positions Trainer – Practice specific pieces, openings, or positions; Focus on areas that need

improvement based on your games, solve positions from Grandmaster games

6.  Visualization Trainer -

a)  Square Trainer – Tests your quick recognition and helps you identify the squares by name
b)  Calculation Trainer – Presents you with positions and asks you to visualise future moves without

moving the pieces
Intuition Trainer – You can guess Grandmaster moves to develop a Champion’s instinct
