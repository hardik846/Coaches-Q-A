🎯 Purpose of PTM

The goal of the Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM) is to ensure clear communication between coach and parent
regarding:

Student’s current progress
•
Strengths and improvement areas
•
•  Learning behavior and engagement
•  Clear action plan for the next phase

👉   By the end of the PTM, the parent should clearly understand:
How my child is doing → What needs improvement → What happens next

For 1-on-1 Classes:

For 1-on-1 students, the 8th session itself will be a combined Test and PTM session. There will be no
separate PTM booking for 1-on-1 students.

During this session, the coach will conduct a short assessment or test, share the student’s progress
and feedback with the parent, and explain the focus areas for the next phase.

 This session will be treated as a regular 1-on-1 class, and no additional PTM payment will be
applicable.

⏱️ 10-Minute Structured PTM Framework

🕐 0:00 – 1:00 | Warm Opening

“Hi Mr./Mrs. [Name], thank you for joining. I’ll quickly share how your child is doing, what’s going well,
where we can improve, and our plan for the next month.”

🕐 1:00 – 4:00 | Progress & Positives

Focus on:

•  Topics covered
Strengths
•
Participation / discipline
•

🕐 4:00 – 6:30 | Improvement Areas

Focus on:

•

1–2 key gaps only
(Examples: board vision, practice, focus, speed)

🕐 6:30 – 9:00 | 30-Day Action Plan

Must include:

•  What coach will teach
•  What student should practice
•  What parent can support

🕐 9:00 – 10:00 | Closing

“Does this plan sound okay to you?
If we follow this consistently, you should see clear improvement next month. Thank you for your support.”

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is the PTM mandatory for coaches?
Yes. Once a PTM is booked by the parent, the coach must attend and conduct it.

2. What if a parent does not join a PTM after the 9th session?
Coaches should inform student to reschedule the PTM via the Caissa platform / respective PTM whatsapp
message. They will get 7 days time to reschedule or else it will get skipped for the month.

3. How will the PTM be conducted?
Online via Caissa platform (same as regular class join flow).

4. Will PTMs be counted as regular classes?
No. PTMs are separate (except for 1-on-1 sessions).

5. How long should PTM last?
Around10 minutes (structured and concise).

6. What should be discussed?

•  Topics covered
Strengths
•
Improvement areas
•
•  Engagement
•  Next phase plan

7. How does PTM work for 1-on-1 sessions?
The 8th session itself is a combined Test + PTM. No separate booking.

8. How long do coaches have to wait for parent to join? Will the coach be paid?
If the coach waits for 5 minutes, and follows the process, 50% payout will be provided.
